Before You Apply

Questions

If you're still wondering, you're in the right place.

Is Pakistan safe?
Pakistan has a reputation that doesn't match the reality of where we're going. Gilgit-Baltistan — the northern region this expedition rides through — is among the most visited and welcoming parts of the country. Tourist arrivals have increased significantly in recent years. We monitor conditions continuously and work with local partners who know this terrain intimately. If you're reading this and part of you is hoping it's a little dangerous — you're wrong. But we like you. You're probably the right person for this trip.
What riding experience do I need?
A valid motorcycle license is required — no exceptions. Beyond that, we're looking for honest self-assessment over impressive credentials. You should be comfortable on roads that aren't smooth, in conditions that aren't predictable, for long days at altitude. When in doubt, tell us the truth on the application. We'd rather help you prepare than have the wrong person on the road.
What bike will I be on?
All riders are on 150cc bikes provided through Adventure Planners Pakistan, who have over ten years of experience on this specific route. These bikes are purpose-selected for the Karakoram — nimble, reliable, and well-suited to the road conditions. A mechanic rides along in the support vehicle for the full 15 days.
What fitness level is required?
You don't need to be an athlete. You need to be someone who doesn't quit when things get uncomfortable. Beyond the riding, the expedition includes optional hikes ranging from moderate to tough — some of the most jaw-dropping views on the entire trip are only accessible on foot. A basic cardio base helps at altitude.
Do I need my own gear?
Helmet and jacket are mandatory on the road. Riding boots and protective pants are strongly recommended. You can bring your own or we can advise on rental options. Custom Junoon Journeys riding jackets are in development — available to rent or purchase. Gear is not included in the expedition price.
What's the weather like?
September and October are the best months to ride this route. Days are warm and clear, nights cool at altitude. Hunza in full autumn colour is one of the most visually striking things you will see in your life. At Khunjerab Pass at 4,693m it will be cold regardless — bring layers, seriously. A full packing guide is included in the information packet.
Do I need a visa?
Most nationalities require a visa to enter Pakistan. Many countries are eligible for visa on arrival or e-visa. We offer visa processing assistance at additional cost — note your nationality on the application and we'll advise. Start early. Pakistani visa processing times vary and we don't want it to be the thing that stops you.
What if something goes wrong mechanically?
A 4x4 support vehicle with an experienced mechanic follows the group throughout the entire route. Adventure Planners Pakistan has been running these roads for over a decade — mechanical issues are anticipated, not emergencies. You will not be left on the side of a mountain pass alone.
Can I join solo?
Yes. Most riders join solo. The group dynamic is part of the experience — you will not feel alone for long.
What is the group like?
Up to 10 riders per expedition. We select based on fit, experience, and group balance — not first come first served. You won't all be the same type of person. That's intentional.
When will I hear back?
Within 5 business days. Every application is reviewed personally. Contact: saad@junoonjourneys.com
I've never traveled to Pakistan — what do I need to know?
More than we can fit here. Accepted riders receive a detailed information packet covering Pakistani culture, history, the north specifically, food, language basics, how to dress respectfully, and everything practical you need before arrival. The short version: be curious, be humble, say yes to the chai.
